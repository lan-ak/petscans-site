Is Avocado safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it Caution for both dogs and cats.

DogsCaution
CatsCaution

What is Avocado?

A fatty fruit. Note: It contains persin and that some pet foods use avocado oil safely.

Why is it in pet food?

Included as a fruit ingredient.

What to watch for

Warning For dogs and cats

Avocado contains persin which can cause mild GI upset. The pit is a choking hazard. Some pet foods use avocado oil safely.

Evidence: medium · Source: ASPCA Animal Poison Control

How common is it in pet food?

Avocado appears in 98 of the 25,052 dog and cat foods and treats PetScans tracks (68 for dogs, 30 for cats).
