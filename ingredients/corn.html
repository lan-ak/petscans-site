Is Corn safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it safe for most pets for both dogs and cats.

DogsSafe for most
CatsSafe for most

What is Corn?

A cereal grain, minimally processed. It supplies carbohydrate and linoleic acid, an essential fatty acid.

Why is it in pet food?

Included as a carbohydrate source and for its linoleic acid.

What to watch for

Rated safe for most rather than simply safe, which allows for individual sensitivities.

How common is it in pet food?

Corn appears in 1,938 of the 25,052 dog and cat foods and treats PetScans tracks (1,193 for dogs, 745 for cats). In 1,516 of them it is one of the first five ingredients on the label, which are listed by weight.
