Is Pineapple safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it safe in moderation for both dogs and cats.

DogsIn moderation
CatsIn moderation

What is Pineapple?

A tropical fruit containing bromelain, an enzyme that breaks down protein.

Why is it in pet food?

Included for its enzymes and vitamins.

What to watch for

Rated safe in moderation. It is high in sugar, and the skin and core should be removed.

How common is it in pet food?

Pineapple appears in 130 of the 25,052 dog and cat foods and treats PetScans tracks (96 for dogs, 34 for cats). In 8 of them it is one of the first five ingredients on the label, which are listed by weight.
