Is Onion powder safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it Avoid for both dogs and cats.

DogsAvoid
CatsAvoid

What is Onion powder?

Dehydrated onion. Note: This concentrated form is more toxic per gram than fresh onion.

Why is it in pet food?

Included as a flavouring in the recipe.

What to watch for

Critical For dogs and cats

Onion powder is a concentrated form of onion - more toxic per gram than fresh onion. Can cause hemolytic anemia.

Evidence: strong · Source: ASPCA Animal Poison Control

How common is it in pet food?

Onion powder appears in 21 of the 25,052 dog and cat foods and treats PetScans tracks (21 for dogs, 0 for cats).
