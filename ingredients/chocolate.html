Is Chocolate safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it Avoid for both dogs and cats.

DogsAvoid
CatsAvoid

What is Chocolate?

A confection made from cocoa. Note: It contains theobromine and that dark chocolate is most dangerous.

Why is it in pet food?

Present as a flavouring in the product.

What to watch for

Critical For dogs

Chocolate contains theobromine which is highly toxic to dogs. Dark chocolate and baking chocolate are most dangerous. Contact your vet immediately if ingested.

Evidence: strong · Source: ASPCA Animal Poison Control

Critical For cats

Chocolate contains theobromine which is toxic to cats. While cats rarely eat chocolate, any ingestion is dangerous.

Evidence: strong · Source: ASPCA Animal Poison Control

Signs of a problem can include: vomiting, diarrhea, excessive thirst, restlessness, tremors, seizures, abnormal heart rhythm.

  • Dogs: 1 oz milk chocolate per lb body weight potentially fatal
  • Cats: Less commonly poisoned but equally susceptible

How common is it in pet food?

Chocolate appears in 1 of the 25,052 dog and cat foods and treats PetScans tracks (1 for dogs, 0 for cats).
