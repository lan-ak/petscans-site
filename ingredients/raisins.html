Is Raisins safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it Avoid for dogs and Caution for cats.

DogsAvoid
CatsCaution

What is Raisins?

Dried grapes. Note: Drying concentrates the risk further.

Why is it in pet food?

Present as a dried fruit in the product.

What to watch for

Critical For dogs

Raisins are dried grapes and are even more toxic per gram than fresh grapes. Can cause acute kidney failure in dogs.

Evidence: strong · Source: ASPCA Animal Poison Control

Signs of a problem can include: vomiting, diarrhea, lethargy, anorexia, abdominal pain, kidney failure.

  • Dogs: 1 raisin per 4.5 kg body weight may cause kidney injury
  • Cats: Unknown - limited data

How common is it in pet food?

Raisins appears in 3 of the 25,052 dog and cat foods and treats PetScans tracks (3 for dogs, 0 for cats). In 1 of them it is one of the first five ingredients on the label, which are listed by weight.
