Is Macadamia nuts safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it Avoid for dogs and Safe for cats.

DogsAvoid
CatsSafe

What is Macadamia nuts?

A rich, oily tree nut used in confectionery and baking.

Why is it in pet food?

Present as a nut ingredient in the product.

What to watch for

Critical For dogs

Macadamia nuts cause weakness, vomiting, tremors, and hyperthermia in dogs. Symptoms usually appear within 12 hours.

Evidence: strong · Source: ASPCA Animal Poison Control

Signs of a problem can include: weakness, incoordination, depression, vomiting, tremors, hyperthermia.

  • Dogs: 2.4g/kg body weight causes symptoms
  • Cats: Not known to be toxic to cats

How common is it in pet food?

Macadamia nuts does not appear on any of the 25,052 dog and cat food labels PetScans tracks, but it can turn up in homemade food, table scraps and some treats.
