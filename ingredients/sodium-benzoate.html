Is Sodium benzoate safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it Caution for both dogs and cats.

DogsCaution
CatsCaution

What is Sodium benzoate?

A synthesised salt used to stop microbes growing.

Why is it in pet food?

Added as an antimicrobial preservative.

What to watch for

Info For dogs and cats

Sodium benzoate is an antimicrobial preservative. Some pets may be sensitive to it. Generally considered safe in small amounts.

Evidence: medium · Source: FDA GRAS

How common is it in pet food?

Sodium benzoate appears in 67 of the 25,052 dog and cat foods and treats PetScans tracks (61 for dogs, 6 for cats). In 2 of them it is one of the first five ingredients on the label, which are listed by weight.
