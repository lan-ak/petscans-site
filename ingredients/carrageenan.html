Is Carrageenan safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it Caution for both dogs and cats.

DogsCaution
CatsCaution

What is Carrageenan?

A gelling agent extracted from red algae, used mainly in wet food to set its texture.

Why is it in pet food?

Added as a gelling agent, giving canned food its consistency.

What to watch for

Rated as caution and notes that some owners avoid it because of gastrointestinal sensitivity.

How common is it in pet food?

Carrageenan appears in 1,843 of the 25,052 dog and cat foods and treats PetScans tracks (883 for dogs, 960 for cats). In 57 of them it is one of the first five ingredients on the label, which are listed by weight.
