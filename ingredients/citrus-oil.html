Is Citrus oil safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it Caution for dogs and Avoid for cats.

DogsCaution
CatsAvoid

What is Citrus oil?

An essential oil from citrus peel. Note: It contains limonene and linalool.

Why is it in pet food?

Included as an essential oil, typically for scent.

What to watch for

Critical For cats

Citrus oils contain limonene and linalool which cats cannot metabolize. Can cause liver damage and CNS depression.

Evidence: strong · Source: ASPCA Animal Poison Control

Signs of a problem can include: vomiting, diarrhea, CNS depression, liver damage.

  • Dogs: Use with caution
  • Cats: Any amount - cats lack enzymes to metabolize

How common is it in pet food?

Citrus oil does not appear on any of the 25,052 dog and cat food labels PetScans tracks, but it can turn up in homemade food, table scraps and some treats.

Is Citrus oil safe for cats?

PetScans rates it Avoid for cats. Citrus oils contain limonene and linalool which cats cannot metabolize. Can cause liver damage and CNS depression. Reported toxic dose: Any amount - cats lack enzymes to metabolize.

Why is Citrus oil in pet food?

Included as an essential oil, typically for scent.
