Is Bananas safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it safe in moderation for both dogs and cats.

DogsIn moderation
CatsIn moderation

What is Bananas?

A fruit used in small amounts, contributing potassium and fibre.

Why is it in pet food?

Included as a source of potassium and fibre, often in treats.

What to watch for

Rated safe in moderation, noting they are high in sugar and should be used sparingly.

How common is it in pet food?

Bananas appears in 420 of the 25,052 dog and cat foods and treats PetScans tracks (378 for dogs, 42 for cats). In 86 of them it is one of the first five ingredients on the label, which are listed by weight.
