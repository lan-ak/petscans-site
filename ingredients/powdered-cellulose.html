Is Powdered cellulose safe for dogs and cats?

PetScans rates it Safe for both dogs and cats.

DogsSafe
CatsSafe

What is Powdered cellulose?

Purified plant fibre, ground to a powder. Our data describes it as non-digestible and notes its use in weight management formulas.

Why is it in pet food?

Included as insoluble fibre and as a low-calorie filler that adds bulk without adding energy.

How common is it in pet food?

Powdered cellulose appears in 2,008 of the 25,052 dog and cat foods and treats PetScans tracks (1,065 for dogs, 943 for cats). In 387 of them it is one of the first five ingredients on the label, which are listed by weight.
